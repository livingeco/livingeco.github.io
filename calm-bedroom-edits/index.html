Start With One

The bedroom you'll look forward to

If I were starting from scratch, I'd begin with linen bedding or a warmer bedside lamp. They're the two changes you feel the very first night, and everything else can be layered in slowly from there. You don't need to do all ten at once. Pick one, notice how it feels, then add the next.

A Few Questions

Before you start

Do I need to buy all ten upgrades at once?

No. Pick the one that solves your most immediate problem, whether that's poor sleep, harsh lighting, or a bed that never looks made, and add the rest over time.

What's the single highest-impact upgrade on this list?

Linen bedding and blackout curtains have the biggest effect on how well you actually sleep. Bedside lighting has the biggest effect on how the room feels.

Are these upgrades renter-friendly?

Yes. Everything on this list is freestanding, clip-on, or plug-in. Nothing requires painting, drilling, or a landlord's permission.

How is this different from a bedroom decorating guide?

This isn't about how the room looks in photos. It's about how the room feels at 9pm on a Tuesday, which is a different, more useful question.
